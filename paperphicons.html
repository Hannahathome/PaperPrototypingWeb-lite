Paper Prototyping Lite PaperBlox

PaperPhicons

A paper prism or frustum with 3 to 24 sides, with lids, a colour and images. Print at actual size (100 %, no "fit to page"), then cut the fold file and fold along the dashed lines.

Shape

For 4 sides you enter the side length, so 30 gives a 30 × 30 mm square. For any other number of sides you enter the diameter of the circle through the corners.

Lids

Without a lid the shape stays open at that end; the glue tabs on the side strip stay.

Colour and images

Side images read upright on the folded shape. Panel images go around the folded shape from left to right in the order you picked them. A fill colour prints over images. Images stay on your computer; nothing is uploaded.
